How the workflow works

1

Inquiry arrives

A visitor submits service details, contact information, and a preferred time.

2

Receipt is sent

The visitor receives a clear confirmation and the next step, such as a booking link.

3

Owner is alerted

A concise summary is added to the pipeline so the right person can respond.

4

Follow-up is visible

A next-action time is created. The owner can update the status after speaking with the lead.

Human-in-the-loop by design

The workflow does not quote jobs, diagnose problems, or make promises to customers. It handles acknowledgement, organization, and reminders while the business owner controls pricing, scheduling, and service decisions.

This is a demonstration. The actual client workflow is configured and tested against the client’s forms, inbox, and policies before any production use.
