# Founding Offer: Lead Response Recovery Sprint

## Positioning

I help small home-service companies respond to new inquiries faster and follow up consistently, using the tools they already have. The work is a practical workflow implementation, not generic AI consulting.

> **Outcome:** Every new web inquiry is acknowledged, organized, and assigned a human follow-up action instead of disappearing into an inbox.

## Why this niche and problem

The 2026 Federal Reserve report on employer firms found that reaching customers and growing sales was the most common operational challenge. The same report found that 46% of employer firms were already using AI, but only 7% of AI users had fully integrated it into business processes; adapting tools to business needs was one of the leading challenges.[1] Home-service customer research also emphasizes speed, communication, online convenience, proactive updates, and follow-up as important parts of the hiring experience.[2] This makes a narrow implementation offer more credible than a broad “AI agency” pitch.

## The paid pilot

For the first two founding clients, the fixed-scope pilot is **$149 USD** or the local-currency equivalent, paid before implementation begins. The pilot is designed to take two business days after the client provides access to the relevant form, spreadsheet, email account, and booking link.

| Included | Delivered result |
|---|---|
| Current-process review | A one-page map of where inquiries currently arrive and where follow-up breaks |
| Lead capture workflow | A structured form or existing-form connection into a simple pipeline sheet |
| Immediate acknowledgement | A customized email confirming receipt and offering the preferred next step |
| Owner alert | A lead summary with service, contact details, and a simple priority score |
| Follow-up queue | A timestamped next-action field and one tested reminder workflow |
| Testing and handoff | Five test submissions, a short operating guide, and a recorded walkthrough or live handoff |

The pilot does not include paid software subscriptions, bulk outreach, automatic quoting, legal or medical workflows, complex CRM migration, or guaranteed revenue results. Any third-party subscription or messaging cost is approved and paid by the client separately.

## Optional add-on

After the pilot works, the client may purchase a **Quote Follow-Up Add-on** for **$99–$199**, depending on the existing tools. This adds a simple follow-up sequence for inquiries that received a quote but have not replied, with clear stop conditions and human review. It is offered only after the first workflow is tested.

## Proof the prospect can inspect

The public proof demo contains the Apps Script code, a readable workflow description, a test-mode safety switch, and a clear explanation of what is and is not automated. There are no fake client results or invented testimonials. A prospect can request a five-minute screen walkthrough or a personalized mini-audit based on their public inquiry process.

## Mini-audit promise

The first conversation should produce one concrete observation, such as: “Your website offers a contact form, but the confirmation does not tell the visitor what happens next,” or “The inquiry path has no visible booking option.” The audit should be based on the prospect’s public website or business listing and should not claim to know internal response times without asking.

## Suggested qualification questions

Ask how new inquiries currently arrive, who responds, how quickly a response normally happens during busy periods, whether follow-up is tracked, and what booking or quoting tool is already in use. A good prospect receives regular inquiries, has a reachable owner or manager, and can approve a small implementation quickly.
